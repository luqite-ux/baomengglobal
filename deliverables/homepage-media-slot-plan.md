2. Product families — representative real product cut-outs for Fasteners and Precision-Machined Parts; 4:3 desktop, square mobile; link to non-empty category filters.
3. Manufacturing footprint — supplied factory entrance and workshop images; asymmetric editorial grid; link to `/manufacturing`.
4. Capability metrics — no invented imagery; restrained technical diagram treatment for 2,000㎡, stated capacity and machining envelope.
5. Custom manufacturing — real shaft/flange/coupling imagery with a drawing-to-delivery flow; CTA `/contact`.
6. Applications — use verified product imagery and semantic line graphics for automotive, machinery, electronics/electrical and industrial manufacturing; never present them as named customer cases.
7. Quality — real inspection/manufacturing imagery; ISO 9001 may be stated only as supplied text, without fabricating a certificate image.
8. FAQ/procurement — text-first accordion sourced from the customer workbook; no warranty content.
9. Company story — supplied office/factory photos; link `/about`.
10. News — backend-ready empty state only; no fabricated articles.
11. Contact/RFQ — balanced company summary, address, phone and inquiry form; avoid a short empty sidebar beside a tall form.
