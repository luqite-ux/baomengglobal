# Motion Plan

- Hero: restrained depth shift and copy reveal; no particles, no black theme. Manual controls, keyboard/touch support, pause and reduced-motion fallback.
- Product families: subtle perspective/edge-light response tied to real metal geometry; no continuous rotation.
- Manufacturing: viewport-triggered stagger and shallow parallax on real workshop imagery.
- Capability flow: SVG line path connecting drawing, forming/machining, inspection and delivery; static readable fallback.
- Every major public-page section animates when it actually enters the viewport. No fixed/global timeout may mark off-screen sections complete.
- Mobile 390px: eliminate expensive parallax/3D, keep opacity/translate transitions under 450ms, preserve tap targets and reading order.
